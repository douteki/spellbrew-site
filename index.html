Every word is a spell.

A word game for Discord. No spellcheck, no timer. Play real words, bluff fake ones, and catch your friends lying.

Add to Discord Play in our server Free to play. 2–4 players.

How it works

For 2 to 4 brewers. Take your time.

You’ll need

  • A Discord server
  • A friend or three
  • A little nerve

Method

  1. Tap New game and pick who’s playing.
  2. Take turns placing words. Any word goes.
  3. Think a word is fake? Hit Challenge. Fakes lose their points. Wrong guesses lose a turn.
  4. At the end, The Trials reveal every bluff nobody caught.

No rush

Take your turn whenever you like. The bot reminds whoever’s up next.

Real words come from the official tournament word lists. US and UK spellings both count.

Full Moon Event

Once a month the cauldron transforms under the light of the full moon. Some words score double, potions can turn wild, and a werewolf hides among the players.
